Case Studies

Three problems, diagnosed and treated

CASE No. 01
Carebotton Inc.
Director / Product Lead
2023 — Present · Canada

Turning home-care visits into a medication-adherence product

The gap

Home care for patients on multi-drug regimens ran on memory and phone calls. Missed doses and care hand-offs weren't visible until something went wrong.

What I did

Spent 20+ hours a week in direct patient care, then converted what I observed into three user personas, a prioritized backlog of 15+ user stories, and three KPIs — adherence rate, care response time, service quality. Validated all of it with patients, families, and care providers before it went near a roadmap.

Result

Two product concepts moved from idea to prioritized backlog, and average care response time improved by an estimated 15%.

CASE No. 02
Reckitt
Product Analyst / Medical Sales Rep
Jan — Jul 2022 · Nigeria

Closing the visibility gap between the field and the dashboard

The gap

Territory performance data existed, but it wasn't reaching the people deciding where to focus next — sales, marketing, and clinical teams were working from instinct.

What I did

Built Power BI and Excel dashboards tracking product performance across the territory. Sat through 30+ healthcare-provider conversations and turned the recurring feedback into requirements for product positioning and messaging, segmented by provider group.

CASE No. 03
Medplus Health & Beauty
Operations Manager, Pharmacy
Mar 2018 — Jan 2022 · Nigeria

Redesigning a pharmacy workflow around the patient, not the counter

The gap

A team of 8+ was absorbing wait-time complaints and workflow bottlenecks without a structured way to diagnose where the friction actually lived.

What I did

Ran daily stand-ups to keep execution aligned to goals, mapped the workflow to find the actual bottlenecks, and set monthly targets across inventory turnover, service time, and patient satisfaction — collecting feedback from 100+ patient interactions a month to keep the targets honest.

Result

Patient wait times fell by an estimated 20%, and service delivery held that improvement across a four-year tenure.

8+Team Members
100+Monthly Patient Touchpoints
~20%Shorter Wait Times*

* Figures are directional estimates based on documented outcomes — swap in verified numbers before sharing with employers.
